ABOUT ZEE & DEE

What Brings Us Together?

Zee & Dee is more than a collection of business. It is a place where people can eat, shop, relax, connect and celebrate ~ all in one welcoming corner.

What started as a neighbourhood shop has grown into one of Abuja's favourite destinations: a cafe, a lounge, a carefully curated wines and spirits shop, a home decorations store for house essentials and perfume and scents store. One address many reasons to stay.
